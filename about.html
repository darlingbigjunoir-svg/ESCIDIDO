Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Excellence

Escondido was founded with one simple mission: to provide homeowners and businesses with plumbing services they can genuinely rely on — no hidden fees, no guesswork, no delays.

What started as a small local team has grown into a full-service plumbing company, serving thousands of satisfied clients across the region. We combine modern diagnostic tools, high-grade materials, and precision repair methods to deliver results that last.

Every technician on our team is fully licensed, background-checked, and trained to handle everything from emergency leak repairs to complete bathroom renovations.
